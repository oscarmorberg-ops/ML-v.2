# CISM Domain 2: Information Risk Management

## Risk Assessment (7 Elements Framework)
1. ✅ Context & Purpose (ML Risk Scoring v5)
2. Scope & Charter (4 prod AWS buckets)
3. Authority Structure (CSIO reporting)
4. Asset Classification (S3 + GuardDuty)
5. Risk Objectives (>7.5/10 = High)
6. Methodology (NIST 800-53 mapping)
7. Implementation Team (GitHub Actions)

## Risk Response Strategy
- **Avoid**: Public S3 buckets
- **Mitigate**: GuardDuty + Macie PII  
- **Transfer**: Cyber insurance eval
- **Accept**: Risk Appetite <7.5/10 ✅

**KRIs (Key Risk Indicators):**
- ML Risk Score >7.5/10
- GuardDuty findings/week
- CloudTrail audit coverage
