# CISM Domain 2: Information Risk Management

## Risk Assessment Process
**7 Elements Risk Program:**
1. Context & Purpose ✅
2. Scope & Charter  
3. Authority Structure ← NEXT
4. Asset Identification
5. Risk Objectives
6. Methodology
7. Implementation Team

## Risk Response Options
- **Avoid**: Zero trust S3 policy
- **Mitigate**: GuardDuty + ML Scoring  
- **Transfer**: Cyber insurance
- **Accept**: Risk Appetite <7.5/10 ✅

## Continuous Monitoring
- KRIs: ML Risk Score >7.5/10
- Key metrics: GuardDuty findings
- Audit trail: CloudTrail logs
